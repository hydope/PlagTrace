Security, author, and contact

  • Author: PlagTrace is built and maintained by hydope on GitHub.
  • Contact / issues: github.com/hydope/PlagTrace/issues — use it for bugs, false positives, and security reports. Do not paste a student's private document into a public issue; describe the problem with a short, anonymised example instead.
  • No account, no storage: the local check stores nothing on any server. Your documents stay in your browser tab and disappear when you close it. Exported JSON reports are created on your device and downloaded by you.
  • Internet check: only short search phrases leave the browser, only after you click "Search internet sources". See Internet check.
  • Code: fully client-side HTML/CSS/vanilla JavaScript, MIT licensed, inspectable in the repo. No trackers, no analytics, no paid APIs in the local check.
  • What to do if you find a wrong result: check the matched passage and its source in the result view, record a reviewer decision, and report the pair (anonymised) as an issue — false positives are how the test set in Accuracy grows.
