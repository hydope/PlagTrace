Check similarity, not guilt.

Paste or upload essays, papers, and academic drafts. PlagTrace shows, source by source, exactly which passages match, how many words matched, and what was excluded — all on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

Scope, stated up front: PlagTrace checks only the documents you supply. It does not search the web, Turnitin's database, or any publisher database. A "no match" here means "no match in your supplied documents" — never "no match anywhere".

This is a screening aid. Only a human reviewer may decide misconduct, after giving the student a chance to respond.
