Why there is no "AI %" here

PlagTrace deliberately contains no AI-text detection. That is not a missing feature — it is a safety decision, and the evidence for it is:

  • Liang et al., Patterns (2023): 7 detectors tested on 91 TOEFL essays by non-native English writers gave a 61.3% average false-positive rate, versus near-zero on native-speaker essays. The detectors were measuring vocabulary simplicity, not authorship.
  • Turnitin's own figures: under 1% false positives only for documents with over 20% AI text, and it likely misses about 15% of AI text to keep false positives low.
  • Independent benchmarks disagree wildly with vendor claims, and detectors disagree with each other on identical human text.
  • OpenAI discontinued its own AI Text Classifier in July 2023 for low accuracy.

An AI % that is wrong about a non-native speaker's essay can end a student's career. A similarity percentage, by contrast, is auditable: you can see the matched passage, its source, and its offsets, and a human can decide what it means.

What PlagTrace shows instead: similarity coverage % per source — matched characters ÷ Document A characters, under stated settings. Every number on a result page can be traced to a visible passage.
